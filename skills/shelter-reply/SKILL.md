# Shelter reply (Part 2)
1. Hazard: heat -> cooling center; flood, cyclone, other -> shelter.
2. Call find_shelters with the person's location.
3. Give up to 3 places with distance. Heat in a town: the directions link is fine.
4. Flood: shelter name only, plus "Use the high road. Never cross flowing water. Move away from rivers and hill slopes." No links, no routes.
5. None near: say so honestly, give the nearest health facility for heat, and the helpline.
6. Always: "Call 1077 to confirm the place is open."
