# Drought and dry-spell advice (Part 1)
Steps from ICAR-CRIDA district contingency plans and KVK advisories. CONFIRM per crop.

1. Say how long the dry spell is expected to last and how sure we are. Drought outlooks beyond 2 weeks are low-confidence: say so.
2. Options by water source:
   - With water (well, canal, drip): protective irrigation now, mulching to keep soil moisture.
   - Without water: mulching; for sowing, wait for enough rain or choose a hardier crop.
3. Always: water and fodder for livestock.
4. Kisan Call Centre for crop advice: 1800-180-1551.
5. Calm tone: some farmers are in distress. Never blame. Options, not orders.
