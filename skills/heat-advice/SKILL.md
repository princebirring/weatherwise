# Heat advice (Part 1)
Steps come from official guidance (NDMA heat-wave guidelines, Maharashtra heat action plan, IMD/KVK agromet advisories). CONFIRM wording against those sources.

1. Say what, where, when, how bad, and how sure (e.g. "7 of 10 forecasts agree"). Use only numbers from hazard_outlook.
2. If the rule is marked "ours", say it is our own early signal and to follow IMD if IMD has issued a warning.
3. Give 2-3 options that fit the person (their crop and water source if known):
   - People who must keep working: work 6-10 am and after 5 pm; shade every hour; water often.
   - Elderly, pregnant women, children, people who are ill: stay indoors 12-4 pm.
   - Cattle and poultry: shade and extra water.
4. Heatstroke signs: confusion, no sweating, fainting. Cool the person and call 108.
5. Offer: "Reply SHELTER for the nearest cooling place."
6. Calm tone. Options, not orders. Short.
