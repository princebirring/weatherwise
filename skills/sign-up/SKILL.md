# Sign-up
1. Consent first: "Can we send you heat, drought and flood warnings? Reply YES." Nothing else before YES.
2. Language: Marathi, Hindi or English.
3. Village: ask them to share their location, or type/say the village name. For a name, call find_place and read the options back: "Wadgaon, Latur, Maharashtra: correct?" Never guess.
4. Role: farmer, household, officer, or family member registering for a relative's village.
5. Farmers: main crop (local names are fine: harbhara, kapus, tur) and water source (well, canal, drip, rain only).
6. Households: near a river, nala or low area? On or below a hill slope?
7. Confirm what they will get, and that STOP ends messages and DELETE removes their data.
