# All-clear (Part 1)
People stop trusting warnings that never end. When our own signals no longer show the risk we
warned about, say so, without ever saying they are safe: officials may still see what we miss.

1. Name the earlier warning from earlier_warnings (what and the day it was sent), and say our latest forecasts no longer show it.
2. Never say "safe", "no danger" or "nothing to worry": say the risk "has eased in our forecasts".
3. Say to keep following IMD and local officials, who may still have warnings out.
4. After a flood or heavy rain: do not go back into flood water or through flowing water; if water is still standing or someone is unwell, call the disaster helpline.
   After heat: keep drinking water; heatstroke can still happen on hot afternoons; call 108 for heatstroke.
5. Two or three short sentences. Calm tone.
